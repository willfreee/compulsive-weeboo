# willfreee from weeboo

The easy way to stop compulsive manga, anime, web novel, light novel, and visual novel bingeing without willpower, timers, or feeling deprived.

## About the book

If you regularly find yourself staring at a screen at three in the morning with stinging eyes, skimming machine-translated chapters you do not even respect, or rushing through *Slam Dunk* and *Vinland Saga* when your alarm goes off in four hours, this book was written for you.

Most advice about screen addiction assumes people only get hooked on brainless garbage. This book looks honestly at both sides of the habit:

1. The slop treadmill: spending hours on copy-paste cultivation novels, generic isekai light novels, seasonal anime watched on double speed, and mindless visual novel route grinding just to keep boredom away.
2. The masterpiece hijack: devouring extraordinary art while exhausted in the dark, turning five-star storytelling into a rushed plot summary and ruining the exact experience you wanted to enjoy.

The goal is not to burn your manga collection, delete your watchlist, or pretend fiction has no artistic value. The goal is to take down the compulsion loop completely so you take back your sleep, clear your daytime focus, and enjoy great stories at an unhurried, human pace.

## How it works

Traditional methods tell you to fight urges with strict willpower, screen-time timers, app blockers, or thirty-day streaks. Those approaches almost always collapse because they try to force restraint while you still believe bingeing gives you genuine relaxation, stress relief, or emotional comfort. As long as stopping feels like a painful sacrifice, your willpower eventually runs out.

This book combines insights from three practical frameworks to solve the problem at the root:

* EasyPeasy (Allen Carr's EasyWay): Systematically takes apart the value illusion (the stack of mental excuses telling you that late-night bingeing relaxes you, cures boredom, or helps you sleep). Once you see that the loop creates the exact restlessness it pretends to soothe, the urge drops away naturally.
* AVRT (Rational Recovery by Jack Trimpey): Recognizes the primitive brain as normal biological hardware that learned old habit loops, especially when automatic urges hijack your inner voice using the word "I." This lets you handle sudden cravings calmly without internal warfare.
* Burgeon: Focuses on practical environmental hygiene, present awareness, and avoiding the trap of frantic self-improvement, showing you how to redirect your freed energy into real life without burnout.

## Two rules before reading

To get the most out of this book, follow two basic instructions:

1. Do not skip chapters. Each chapter clears out one specific excuse holding the loop together. Read them in order so the logic builds naturally.
2. Keep your current routine while reading. Do not delete your bookmarks, uninstall your apps, or swear a dramatic midnight oath to quit tomorrow. Trying to starve a habit before you understand it only turns entertainment into forbidden fruit. Keep your normal routine for now, and simply pay attention while you read.
